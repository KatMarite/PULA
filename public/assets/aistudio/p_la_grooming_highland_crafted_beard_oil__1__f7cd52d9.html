Caution & Storage

For external use only. Contains tree nut oils (Sweet Almond). Perform a patch test on your inner forearm 24 hours before first use; discontinue if irritation occurs. Avoid direct eye contact. Store tightly sealed in a cool, dark place away from direct sunlight. Best used within 12 months of opening (12M PAO).

MORNING RITUAL • DAMP APPLICATION

01

Cleanse & Dampen

Pat your beard towel-dry after a warm shower. Slightly damp hair and open pores allow cold-pressed oils to lock in hydration up to twice as effectively.

02

Warm Between Palms

Measure your recommended drops with the glass pipette into your palm. Rub hands together briskly for 3 seconds to awaken the bergamot & cedar notes.

03

Massage & Comb

Work fingers upward into the skin beneath your beard first, then smooth downward through the length with a wooden comb or boar-bristle brush.
